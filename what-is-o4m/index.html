What is O4M?

O4M is a research and standards effort that writes free, public requirements for checking, from outside a company, whether an AI system's records are complete for the scope the operator declared and have not been altered. It is published by StS Forensics Ltd. It is not OpenAI's o4-mini model, and it is not a product.

What O4M publishes

  • O4M Record Tests 2026, v1.0: the draft requirements (1 November 2026).
  • Demonstration code and test vectors: a verifier and frozen test vectors for the draft (12 November 2026). The repository logs comments as GitHub Issues.
  • AI Records Gap Brief v1.7, with evidence table v3.3: a review of EU and UK rules on whether outsiders can check AI records (22 October 2026).
  • The O4M Dispatch: written analysis from the same work.
  • Two free exercises, Ask the Record and Find the Gap, in eight language versions.

What O4M is not

The requirements and the exercises are not products, certificates or advice, and they issue no certificates. The requirements are public and the same for everyone, so paid work never changes what they say. StS may sell implementation and investigation services. It will not certify anyone against requirements it wrote.

The question behind it

When an AI agent acts on someone's behalf and something goes wrong, the evidence is usually a log. A log can be unaltered and still be incomplete. O4M asks how a stranger can check completeness. The short answer and its limits are here.
