Can anyone outside the company check what an AI system did?

AI agents now act on people's behalf, and when something goes wrong the evidence is usually a log. We write public requirements for checking those logs from outside: is the record complete for the scope the operator declared, and has it been altered?

Try it yourself

Two free exercises, about two to five minutes each. No sign-up, no tracking, nothing uploaded.

Ask the Record

Pick the seat you sit in: a user, a company, an engineer, a regulator, an insurer or an auditor. Answer two questions about an AI agent's record. About two minutes each.

Short address: http://AtR.o4m.ai

Find the Gap

Check the activity log of a warehouse delivery agent in three rounds. Find out what a seal can show and what only an outside witness can. About five minutes.

Short address: http://FtG.o4m.ai

Both are available in English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese and Chinese (traditional for Hong Kong and Taiwan, and simplified). English is the version of record. The other seven versions are machine translations that native speakers have not yet checked. The cases are invented, and the exercises are not products, certificates or advice.

Limits

  • Completeness is measured within a declared, independently observable boundary.
  • An event that never reached the record cannot be found from the record alone, so an independent reference is needed.
  • Results state what stayed unobservable.

Why now

For products placed on the EU market on or after 9 December 2026, the revised Product Liability Directive expects courts to be able to order disclosure of relevant evidence. If a defendant fails to disclose it, the product can be presumed defective (Article 10(2)(a)).

Binding logging duties exist, for example EU AI Act Article 12 for high-risk systems, which now apply from 2 December 2027 for Annex III high-risk systems and 2 August 2028 for those covered by Annex I product law. Our review found that, outside financial services, none of the binding EU and UK instruments reviewed clearly requires records an outsider can check for alteration or omission. Logging standards are in progress (ISO/IEC 24970 and prEN 18229-1) and, as at 20 November 2026, our comparison of them is not finished.
