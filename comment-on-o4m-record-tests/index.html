How to comment on the O4M Record Tests

Comments on the O4M Record Tests 2026 draft are open until Tuesday 1 December 2026. Send them by email to comments@sts-forensics.com with the subject line "O4M Record Tests", or open an issue on the code repository for anything about the verifier or test vectors.

What helps most

  • Name the requirement, for example C14, or the test, for example T3.
  • Say what the text says, what you think it should say, and why.
  • If you tried to reproduce a result, say what you ran and what you got.
  • Tell us where a requirement is unclear, even if you do not know the fix.

What happens to your comment

Comments and the editor's replies are kept in a public comment log. Comments may be published with your name unless you ask otherwise. Corrections are published as numbered new versions, and earlier versions stay online.

Where to send what

  • The specification: email comments@sts-forensics.com.
  • The verifier and test vectors: open an issue and choose Comment, Reader report or Repeat run.

Known issues already open for comment

  • Profile W needs its own declaration set and its own bounds.
  • Verifier shopping is not yet prevented. A public register of results for each evidence bundle is under consideration.
  • The result vocabulary will be closed to PASS, FAIL and HOLD, with annotations.
  • The word "gap" has two senses in the text and will be unified.
  • One requirement row carries demonstration state inside its text and will be moved.

The draft

The requirements in plain English, or the full text: 10.5281/zenodo.23857888.

After 1 December 2026 this page will say that the comment window has closed and point to the comment log.
