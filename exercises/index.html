Ask the Record and Find the Gap

Ask the Record and Find the Gap are two free, short exercises that show why the completeness of an AI system's records is hard to judge. They take about two to five minutes each, need no sign-up, track nothing and upload nothing.

Ask the Record

Pick the seat you sit in: a user, a company, an engineer, a regulator, an insurer or an auditor. Answer two questions about an AI agent's record. About two minutes each.

Open Ask the Record Short address: http://AtR.o4m.ai

Find the Gap

Check the activity log of a warehouse delivery agent in three rounds. Find out what a seal can show and what only an outside witness can. About five minutes.

Open Find the Gap Short address: http://FtG.o4m.ai

Languages

Both are available in English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese and Chinese (traditional for Hong Kong and Taiwan, and simplified), which makes eight language versions. English is the version of record. The other seven versions are machine translations that native speakers have not yet checked.

What they are not

The cases are invented, and the exercises are not products, certificates or advice. The exercises are released under Apache-2.0.

Where they come from

The exercises accompany the draft O4M Record Tests 2026 and the review behind it, AI Records Gap Brief v1.7. For the idea in plain words, read how an outsider can check that an AI log is complete.
